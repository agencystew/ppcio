# Offer Architecture & Risk-Reversal Playbook

Most landing pages do not fail because the CSS is ugly. They fail because the **offer is vague, unpriced, risky, or indistinguishable from 10 competitors**, and founders are too close to see it.

## 1. The 7-Point Offer Teardown

Judge (or rebuild) any paid-search offer against these 7 checks: skipping only the ones that genuinely do not fit the page archetype (e.g., do not force fake urgency on an evergreen brand homepage):

1. **Specific**: Does the buyer know *exactly* what they get, what is included, and how long it takes? (*"Free 45-minute on-site roof inspection with written steel vs. asphalt quote"* beats *"Free consultation"*.)
2. **Priced (or Price-Transparent)**: Is the price, starting price, financing option, or pricing structure clear? Never force a fake fixed price on custom jobs (see Section 2).
3. **Guaranteed / Risk-Reversed**: What happens if they are unhappy, change their mind, or something breaks? Put the real warranty, refund policy, or zero-obligation terms right beside the CTA.
4. **Urgent (Only If Real)**: Real seasonal slots, genuine promo end dates, limited same-day dispatch windows, or next available booking slot. **Never fake countdown timers or invented "only 2 spots left!" theatre.**
5. **Differentiated**: Why choose THIS business over the other 3 Google Ads tabs they just opened? Name the concrete difference (in-house crews vs subcontractors, 120 mph wind rating, fixed upfront quote, 20-year warranty, specialist focus).
6. **Awareness-Matched**: Does the ask match their buying stage? (A cold problem-aware searcher will take a *"Free Roof Inspection"* or *"See Pricing Guide"*; a brand/most-aware searcher wants *"Book Now"* or *"Call Direct"*.)
7. **Bonus-Stacked (Where Natural)**: Valuable extras already included that the business forgets to mention (free haul-away of old unit, free CAD layout, lifetime workmanship warranty, transferable warranty, priority support).

## 2. How to Handle Pricing When Quotes Vary

Never bully a custom-service business into inventing fake flat prices: and never let them hide behind a blank *"Contact Us"* wall either. Use one of these four honest patterns (using ONLY facts the business confirms):

1. **Starting anchor + scope:** *"Systems from £2,495 installed: exact price confirmed after a 20-minute survey."*
2. **Transparent financing / monthly framing:** *"$0 down financing available for qualified homeowners"* (always keep required qualifying disclosures intact).
3. **Price-guarantee / no-surprise framing:** *"Fixed written quote before we start: zero call-out fee and no surprise extras."*
4. **Clear quote mechanics:** Tell the buyer *what determines the price* (roof size, material, pitch) and *how fast* they get a written number (*"2-minute form → written quote within 24 hours"*).

## 3. Risk-Reversal by Ticket Size

Place the strongest true risk-reducer directly under or beside the primary button:

- **Low ticket / E-commerce (<$150):** Clear returns window, free/fast shipping threshold, warranty, real delivery dates.
- **Urgent local trades ($150–$2,000):** No call-out fee, upfront fixed quote before work starts, workmanship guarantee, licensed & insured credentials, arrival window.
- **High-ticket home services ($5,000–$50,000+):** Written no-pressure estimate, transferable product + workmanship warranty, flexible financing, local project photos & verified reviews, proof of insurance/licensing.
- **B2B Services / SaaS:** Low-friction first step (live audit, interactive demo, free trial, pilot), clear "what happens on the 15-minute call" agenda, zero lock-in / cancel-anytime terms, named client outcomes.

## 4. "What Happens Next" Microcopy

Anxiety peaks at the exact moment a buyer hovers over a submit button. One plain line directly beneath the primary CTA lifts completion rates reliably:

- ❌ Button: *"Submit"* (zero microcopy)
- ✅ Button: *"Get My Free Estimate"* + line underneath: *"We'll confirm your preferred time by phone or text: no obligation to buy."*
- ✅ Button: *"Book 15-Minute Demo"* + line underneath: *"See it on your own account. No deck, no hard sell."*
- ✅ Button: *"Call 01onal-555-0199"* + line underneath: *"Direct to our local dispatch team: 2-hour arrival slots today."*

*(Always verify the promised response time/channel against the business's real operations before writing it.)*

## 5. Strict Offer Honesty & Local Prices

- **Never invent** `"free"`, `"no obligation"`, `"money-back guarantee"`, `"24/7"`, `"same-day"`, `"£X"`, or `"[price]"` unless the business explicitly states it. If a strong offer term is missing, recommend it *outside* copy quotes as a conditional suggestion: *"Add your workmanship warranty right under the quote button, if you offer one."*
- **Local currency warning:** Many international stores auto-convert currency by visitor IP. Never accuse a store of "charging US buyers in GBP" based solely on a single-country capture: quote the price as shown and judge the offer's clarity and value.
- **Keep promo dates fresh:** Flag any expired seasonal/year copy (*"Make 2025 the year..."*) immediately: stale promo copy makes a paid landing page look abandoned.
