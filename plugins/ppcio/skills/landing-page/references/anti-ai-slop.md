# Anti-AI-Slop: Copy, Voice & Design Taste

Why most AI-generated landing pages fail in 1 second: and the exact standards that make a page look like an elite studio designer and a sharp founder built it together.

## 1. The 1-Second Visual Bar

A business owner knows in under 1 second whether a page is elite or cheap AI slop. What **10/10** means:
- The owner opens it, sees **their own brand at its absolute best**, and wants to put it live today.
- Every page is unmistakably **that specific business**: never one SaaS/agency template wearing swapped hex colours.
- Real photos, real customer quotes, real typography, generous spacing, and crisp visual hierarchy carry the page.

## 2. Plain Founder Voice (`VOICE:v1`)

Write like a sharp marketer or business owner talking across a desk: never like a pitch deck, Reddit thread, or LLM essay:

1. **Read it out loud.** If a human owner would never say the sentence to a customer, rewrite it until they would.
2. **Short words, short sentences.** Target ~6–10 words per headline, ~13 words per sentence, and 1–3 short sentences per paragraph. Cut every word you can.
3. **Active voice, buyer vocabulary.** Call things what the buyer calls them.
4. **Concrete beats clever.** Name the exact thing, the exact number, or the exact outcome.
5. **Zero em dashes (` - `).** Use a full stop, comma, or colon, or split the sentence.
6. **Zero recycled metaphors or deck words.** Never write: *unlock, leverage, seamless, elevate, robust, empower, streamline, cutting-edge, game-changer, comprehensive, tailored solutions, bespoke excellence, next-level, revolutionize*.
7. **Every heading reads alone.** Assume a visitor reads ONLY the H1 and H2s top to bottom. Never write topic labels (*"How it works"*, *"Why choose us"*, *"Our process"*, *"What our customers say"*, *"What the free assessment covers"*). Write the actual answer or benefit:
   - ❌ *"What the free assessment covers"* → ✅ *"We measure the room first, so the quote holds"*
   - ❌ *"Every fee on both leases"* → ✅ *"$0 down, and every fee shown before you sign"*
   - ❌ *"Why choose us"* → ✅ *"4.8 stars from 40,000+ homeowners: and installed in 1–2 days"*
8. **Never write classic poetic or verbose AI copy:**
   - ❌ Paired dramatic fragments (*"Five tabs, or one trip."*, *"Built for speed. Designed for trust."*, *"Less stress. More results."*, *"One click. Zero friction."*)
   - ❌ Poetic / literary metaphors (*"Reclaim your mornings."*, *"Built to outlast the storm."*, *"Crafted for the way you actually live."*)
   - ❌ Verbose throat-clearing before the point (*"In today's fast-paced world..."*, *"When it comes to protecting your home..."*)
   - ❌ Artificial groups of three (*"Fast, reliable, and affordable."*)
   - ❌ Slogans with no fact, outcome, or buyer problem (*"Where quality meets craftsmanship."*)
   - ✅ **Normal human conversion copy:** Write the exact plain sentence a sharp business owner or top salesperson says on the phone when a customer asks *"Why should I hire you?"*
     - ❌ *"Precision-engineered roofing built for decades of quiet confidence."* → ✅ *"Get a storm-ready roof installed in 1–2 days with a written lifetime warranty."*
     - ❌ *"Your search ends here. Dentistry reimagined around you."* → ✅ *"Same-day emergency dental appointments in Austin, with upfront pricing before we start."*
9. **Break any rule before writing something unnatural.**

## 3. Writing Audit Fixes for Real Business Owners (`FIXLINES:v1`)

When giving recommendations or audit fixes to a client or owner, drop marketing jargon:

- Start with a plain verb: **Put, Add, Move, Cut, Show, Swap, Ask, Write, Replace.**
- Name the actual element on the page: **the headline, the button, the form, the price, your reviews, the photo, the phone number.**
- Keep the first sentence under 120 characters so it stands on its own.
- Say **"the top of the page"** (not *above the fold / hero*), **"the button"** (not *CTA*), **"your reviews"** (not *social proof*).
- Never write *surface (as a verb), beat, stack, identity gap, category-distancing, lever, bridge, mechanism, or funnel* in client-facing fixes.
- Any unconfirmed idea stays a conditional choice at the end (*"Put your refund promise under the button, if you offer one."*).

| ❌ Strategist jargon | ✅ Plain owner fix |
|---|---|
| *Surface 'From £299pp' as a line in the hero* | *Put 'From £299 per person' in the first line at the top of the page.* |
| *Increase social proof near the CTA* | *Put two of your customer reviews next to the 'Book now' button.* |
| *Add a category-distancing beat near the hero* | *Add one line near the top explaining what you do that a cheap package deal does not.* |
| *Your proof shows up too late* | *Your 400 reviews sit below the form, so visitors decide before they see them.* |

## 4. The AI Design Slop Checklist (What to Kill on Sight)

Unless the business's own live brand explicitly uses them, **never** default to these telltale AI-generated design habits:

### Typography & Text
- **Defaulting to serif headlines with italic accent words** on every site: it immediately screams generic AI template. Match the business's real fonts (from `craft.md`) or pick a pairing that genuinely belongs to their trade.
- **Tiny hard-to-read body copy.** Body text must be **17px or larger**; no readable text under 14px.
- **Code-style / monospace fonts** (`JetBrains Mono`, `Fira Code`, `IBM Plex Mono`, `font-family: monospace`) used for marketing eyebrows, stat labels, or prices on non-developer sites.
- **ALL-CAPS screaming headlines** or negative letter-spacing crushed so tight letters collide.
- **Small pill eyebrow badges** above every single headline (`● TRUSTED BY THOUSANDS` / `01 / OUR PROCESS`).

### Layout & Chrome
- **3-column identical icon-card grids** repeated section after section (icon in a tinted circle + bold title + 2 filler lines).
- **Numbered section counters** (`01`, `02`, `03`, `04`) stapled onto every heading or card.
- **Invented off-brand palettes**: defaulting to generic dark navy, sage green, warm sand, or purple/indigo gradients when the business's own brand is bright blue, forest green + lime, red, or crisp white. Their brand colours carry the page; anything added is a quiet neutral or tint of their colours.
- **Glassmorphism, glowing orbs, and ambient radial blur blobs** (`backdrop-filter: blur`, `filter: blur(80px)` purple/cyan halos) behind cards.
- **Box-itis & divider spam**: wrapping every paragraph in a bordered rounded card (`border-radius: 24px` everywhere) and drawing `<hr>` lines between every element.
- **Emoji in headings or bullet lists** (`🚀`, `✨`, `✅`, `💡`) on serious local, B2B, medical, or legal businesses.
- **Fake interactive UI**: tabs, toggles, calculators, or forms that look clickable and do nothing. Every control works, or it becomes clean static copy/imagery.
