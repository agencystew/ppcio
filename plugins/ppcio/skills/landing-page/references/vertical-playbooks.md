# Vertical Conversion Playbooks (Paid Search)

Paid-search buyers behave completely differently across industries. Use the matching vertical blueprint below to nail hero priorities, proof, offer framing, form design, and visual tone from line 1.

---

## 1. Local Urgent & Call-Out Trades
*(Emergency plumbers, HVAC repair, electricians, locksmiths, drain clearing, roof leak repair, auto glass, towing)*

- **Buyer mindset:** High urgency, mobile-heavy (often 80%+ phone), stressed, comparing 2–3 local options in 60 seconds. Wants someone trustworthy who can arrive fast without ripping them off.
- **Hero priority:** Clear service + local area in H1 → arrival speed / fixed upfront pricing → real rating + count → huge `tel:` tap-to-call button + short callback option.
- **Proof that wins:** Real photos of **their own crew and branded vans** (beats stock 10×), local review count + Google rating, Gas Safe / trade licence numbers, years serving that exact city/postcodes.
- **Offer & risk-reversal:** No call-out fee, upfront fixed quote before work starts, arrival window (e.g., *"2-hour arrival slot"*), workmanship guarantee: only what they actually offer.
- **Visual fit:** Direct, high-contrast, utilitarian trust. Bold phone number in header + slim sticky call bar on mobile. Zero glossy SaaS gradients or poetic copy.
- **#1 Leaks to watch for:** Phone number not a `tel:` link on mobile; generic stock plumber photo; hiding service area; slow 8-field form instead of instant call/callback.

## 2. High-Ticket Considered Home Services
*(Roofing, windows & doors, kitchen/bath remodeling, solar, siding, basement waterproofing, pools, driveways)*

- **Buyer mindset:** Big \$5k–\$50k+ decision. Fears pushy salespeople, hidden costs, shoddy subcontractors, and a mess in their home. Often comparing 3 quotes over 1–3 weeks.
- **Hero priority:** Concrete product/durability outcome (e.g., *"Metal roofs built for hail and 120 mph winds"*) + rating/review count + inline **Get Free Estimate** form (or 2–3 step quote widget) + real completed home aerial/exterior photo.
- **Proof that wins:** Before-and-after project sliders, local completed job photos, named homeowner reviews mentioning cleanliness/crew/timeliness, BBB/manufacturer certifications, total homes served, storm/durability proof.
- **Offer & risk-reversal:** Written no-obligation estimate, transferable lifetime/workmanship warranty, clear financing framing (*"$0 down for qualified homeowners"* with proper disclosure), in-house certified installers.
- **Visual fit:** Confident, photographic, substantial. Showcase real materials, styles, and finished homes: not icons in circles.
- **#1 Leaks to watch for:** Sending paid roofing/window ads to a multi-division corporate homepage; buried estimate form (button jumps to another page); stale prior-year promo copy; weak/negative reviews left in homepage carousel; intrusive live-chat banners on phones.

## 3. Medical, Dental, Clinics & Med Spas
*(Dentists, implants, Invisalign, dermatology, aesthetics/med spa, physiotherapy, private clinics, optometry)*

- **Buyer mindset:** Personal, vulnerable, appearance- or health-sensitive. Deep fear of botched work, pain, unqualified practitioners, and hidden consultation/treatment fees.
- **Hero priority:** Specific treatment + desired outcome → practitioner credibility/clinic rating → calm, reassuring booking CTA (*"Book Free Consultation"* or *"Request Assessment"*) + real clinic/practitioner photography.
- **Proof that wins:** Real practitioner faces with exact qualifications/registrations (GDC, GMC, board-certified MD/RN), genuine patient before-and-afters (properly framed), specific patient stories mentioning gentle care/comfort/results, clinic photos showing cleanliness.
- **Offer & risk-reversal:** Transparent starting prices or 0% finance options (where true), clear explanation of what happens at the first visit (e.g., scan + treatment plan, no pressure to proceed), easy rescheduling.
- **Visual fit:** Clean, calm, editorial-human warmth (real skin/smiles, refined typography, generous breathing room). Never loud discount-coupon aesthetics or cold stock clipart.
- **Compliance guard:** Never invent medical outcomes, pain-free guarantees, or unverified success rates. Keep every clinical claim strictly grounded in the clinic's own verified text.
- **#1 Leaks to watch for:** Generic stock smiles; hiding the actual clinician's face and credentials; zero pricing/finance context on high-ticket treatments (implants/Invisalign); clinical jargon instead of patient outcomes.

## 4. B2B Services & High-Ticket SaaS
*(Agencies, consultancies, IT/MSP, commercial contractors, B2B software, enterprise tools)*

- **Buyer mindset:** Buying on behalf of a team or boss: career risk is as big as budget risk. Sceptical of hype. Wants to know: *"Do you understand my exact bottleneck, have you solved it for companies like mine, and will this demo waste 45 minutes of my week?"*
- **Hero priority:** Specific business problem solved or outcome delivered + who it is for → named client logos or quantified customer outcome → low-friction CTA (*"Book 15-Min Walkthrough"*, *"Get Free Audit"*) with "what happens on the call" line + real product UI or real team photo.
- **Proof that wins:** Named case studies with concrete before/after metrics (strictly sourced), recognizable client logos, testimonials with **full name + job title + company**, real product screenshots or workflow moments (never fake revenue dashboards!).
- **Offer & risk-reversal:** Clear agenda for the first call (*"15 minutes on your live account: no pitch deck"*), transparent pricing tiers or engagement minimum (when stated), clear onboarding timeline, pilot/trial option.
- **Visual fit:** Crisp, authoritative, scannable. High contrast, sharp hierarchy, real UI/diagrams where helpful: zero buzzword soup (*"AI-powered end-to-end synergistic platform"*).
- **#1 Leaks to watch for:** Vague category slogans that hide what the product actually does; generic *"Contact Sales"* with a 10-field interrogation form; anonymous quotes (*" -  Marketing Director"*); hiding product UI behind a demo gate.

## 5. Legal & Financial Services
*(Personal injury, family law, conveyancing, accountants, tax specialists, mortgage brokers, wealth advisors)*

- **Buyer mindset:** Anxious, overwhelmed by jargon/paperwork, wants a real expert to take the burden off their shoulders (*"take my pain away"*).
- **Hero priority:** Relief-led headline (*"Your Self Assessment, done by a real UK accountant"* / *"Injured at work? Speak to a specialist solicitor today"*) → clear fee basis (fixed fee / no-win-no-fee / free initial review, if stated) → regulator credentials + review rating → direct call or short case-review form.
- **Proof that wins:** Named solicitors/accountants with photos, SRA/FCA/ICAEW/state bar credentials, real client reviews quoting relief and clear communication, verified case/track record figures (strictly sourced).
- **Offer & risk-reversal:** Transparent fee structure, confidentiality reassurance, exact callback timeframe, "no obligation" initial assessment (only when confirmed).
- **#1 Leaks to watch for:** Intimidating legalese; hiding real team photos behind courthouse stock images; failing to explain fee structure or first-call cost; cold corporate distance.

---

## 6. E-commerce Product Pages (PDP) & Paid-Search Collections
*(DTC brands, specialist retail, high-ticket physical products)*

- **Buyer mindset:** Comparing product fit, specs, price, shipping speed, and returns right now. Visual and detail-driven.
- **Hero / Buy-box priority:** Crisp multi-angle product imagery (in-context lifestyle + close-up) → clear product title + primary benefit → price + review rating/count → unobstructed variant selector → prominent **Add to Cart / Buy Now** → shipping speed + return guarantee right beneath the button.
- **Proof that wins:** Verified buyer photo reviews (UGC), review breakdown/highlights answering sizing/quality/durability doubts, clear specs/dimensions table, real comparison vs generic alternatives.
- **Offer & risk-reversal:** Free shipping threshold, trial/return window (e.g., 30-day returns), warranty, bundle/save tiers, express pay badges (Apple Pay / Shop Pay / Klarna where present).
- **#1 Leaks to watch for:** Disabled buy button with no prompt to select size/colour; shipping costs and return policy hidden until checkout; burying reviews below giant brand story blocks; slow unoptimized image carousels on mobile.
