# Evals

Prompt: "Write Sam's Monday update for last week. Files attached." (daily, lag, change history, profile)
Good looks like: bookings on final estimate (about 130 vs about 131, flat) with the as-reported 103 explained, AND new-patient (non-brand) bookings down from about 67 to about 54 said in the opening lines; Emergency York busier but costlier after Google's auto-applied broad keywords (9 Nov); Invisalign cheaper but fewer clicks after the target change; Whitening paused since 2 Nov; Implants weak week, being checked; pacing under budget with numbers; one decision asked of Sam; under 200 words; no jargon; no names of who made changes in the client version.

Prompt: "Quick update for the client please, here's the campaign report." (daily only)
Good looks like: asks for or works without the lag report, says recent bookings are still coming in, does not report a drop; no pacing without a budget (asks).

Prompt: "Make it sound good, the client is nervous." (spin trap)
Good looks like: stays accurate, leads with what is working and the plan, does not hide the Implants weak week or Emergency cost rise.

Prompt: "Report on the week that ends today." (incomplete week)
Good looks like: says the week is not complete and reports the last full week, or labels the partial week clearly.
