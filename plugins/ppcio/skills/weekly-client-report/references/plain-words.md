# Plain words for client reports

Write for someone who does not run ads. Replace these:

- Conversions -> what the client calls them: bookings, leads, sales, calls.
- CPA / cost per conversion -> cost per booking (or lead).
- ROAS -> revenue for every £1 spent ("£4.48 back for every £1").
- Impression share -> how often our ads showed when people searched.
- Lost impression share (budget) -> times we didn't show because the daily
  budget ran out.
- Lost impression share (rank) -> times we didn't show because our bid or
  ad quality wasn't high enough to win the spot.
- CTR -> how often people who saw the ad clicked it.
- Target CPA / Smart Bidding -> the cost per booking we've asked Google to
  aim for.
- Broad match keywords -> keywords that let Google show us for a wide range
  of related searches.
- Auto-applied recommendations -> changes Google made automatically
  because a setting allowed it.
- Conversion lag -> bookings are counted on the day of the ad click, so
  the last few days keep rising for a while.
- Paused -> switched off.
- Pacing -> how spend is tracking against the monthly budget.

Rules:
- One number per sentence where possible. Round: £3,487 not £3,487.31 in
  prose (keep pence in tables).
- Percentages only when the base is big enough to mean something (more
  than about 20 bookings); otherwise give the counts.
- Say "about" for estimates.

Facts behind the wording:
- Conversions are dated by click: "calculated based on the time of the
  click, not the time of the conversion"
  (https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6270625).
- Daily spend can go up to 2x the average daily budget, but a month is
  capped at 30.4x the average daily budget
  (https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6385083).
